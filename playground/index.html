한국 문화 배경 · why these corners

Every corner starts with one Korean word.

정 (jeong) is the affection that grows from sitting together; 한글 is an alphabet made to be learned in a morning; 복 (bok) is a wish for ordinary luck. Open any corner and a short note under it says which word it carries — and why a Korean would smile at it.

  • 정jeong — the warmth of being together
  • 한han — a quiet, held-back sorrow
  • 복bok — luck in small, ordinary things
  • 눈치nunchi — reading the room

저장과 공유 · keep & share

Keep what you make.

Everything here runs in your own browser — nothing is uploaded and nothing needs an account. So keep it the same way:

  1. 1 · It saves itselfYour wish, hanbok, name card and lists stay in this browser. Come back tomorrow on the same device and they are still there.
  2. 2 · Save the pictureOn a phone, press and hold a finished card → “Save image”, or take a screenshot. On a computer, right-click → “Save image as…”. Recipe cards and colouring sheets print cleanly on A4.
  3. 3 · Share it“Share” copies a link to this corner. “Invite” adds your name to the link, so a friend is greeted by it and you can compare what you both made.

No account, no ads, no upload — the link is the only thing that leaves your device.
