Jeong (정) is the feeling of being quietly looked after.

It is not a grand word. It is the extra egg in your lunchbox, the umbrella left by the door because the forecast said rain, the friend who calls to ask whether you ate. We built this small corner of the internet to give that feeling away — to anyone, anywhere, for free.
