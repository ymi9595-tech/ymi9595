the word behind it all

Jeong (정) is the feeling of being quietly looked after.

It is not a grand word. It is the extra egg in your lunchbox, the umbrella left by the door because the forecast said rain, the friend who texts to ask whether you ate. We built this small corner of the internet to give that feeling away — to anyone, anywhere, for free.

천천히 하세요.
급할 것 없어요.

Take your time. Nothing here is urgent.

— 정
