Small questions

Are these pages really free?

Yes. These three pages are a free sample from the full book — color them on screen, print them, keep them. No account, no email, no watermark.

Can I print them for my class or my family?

The pages come with a personal-use licence: print them for yourself and the people you color with. Schools and care homes are welcome to print a set for a group; reselling the files or the prints is not allowed.

What do I get in the full book?

A 33-page PDF — cover, a short how-to-print page, 30 coloring pages and a closing page. Four seasons: plum blossom lanes, forsythia and cats on stone walls, lotus ponds and pavilions, chili strings and pumpkins, maple stairs, snow on tiled roofs, an ondol room with a teapot.

Does the coloring table work on my phone?

Yes. Tap to fill a region, drag to brush. On a phone the page zooms to fit the screen; turn it sideways for a bigger sheet.
