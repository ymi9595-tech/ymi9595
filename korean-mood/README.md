# Today's Korean Mood — Daily Hangul Cards by DanbiLab

Free, single-page English experience: pick one of six moods and receive a curated Korean
word or line written for that feeling — hangul, romanisation, an English meaning, and a
short note on the culture behind it. Listen to it, save it as a 1080×1350 poster card, copy
it to share.

- Live: <https://ymi9595-tech.github.io/ymi9595/korean-mood/>
- Brand: DanbiLab · <https://danbilab.substack.com>
- Static, no build step, no accounts, no tracking.

## What is on the page

1. **Six moods** (Midnight in Seoul, Rainy & Reflective, Cozy & Slow, Hopeful & New,
   Tired/Need Comfort, Warm Jeong) — 5 curated cards each, 30 in total, cycled without
   repeating until every card in that mood has been seen.
2. **Card stage** — hanji paper card with a dancheong accent per mood: hangul, romanisation,
   English meaning, 2–3 line cultural note, credit line.
3. **Draw another card** (or → arrow key), **Listen** (`speechSynthesis`, `ko-KR`, with
   fallbacks when no Korean voice exists), **Save card** (1080×1350 PNG drawn on canvas),
   **Copy** (share text + link).
4. **Explore more of Korea with DanbiLab** — awareness first: the four free channels (YouTube
   K-Food Farm Kitchen, Seoul Study Café Ambience, Substack, printable K-Food cards) come
   first and are the point of the section. Under them sits a quiet "Full digital guides &
   workbooks" block with the two paid books and no prices or buy buttons in the headline.
   Nothing on the page is gated, and no card asks for an email.

## Poster card

`drawPoster()` renders 1080×1350: paper wash + grain, double gold frame with corner
brackets, 丹 seal, mood label, adaptive hangul sizing (150px down to 78px, up to 2 lines),
italic romanisation, gold rule with a diamond, English meaning, wrapped note (max 6 lines),
and the footer `DanbiLab · danbilab.substack.com`. On iOS the PNG opens in a new tab so it
can be saved to Photos, since blob downloads are ignored there.
